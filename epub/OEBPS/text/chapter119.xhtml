Chapter One Hundred Nineteen

Keep the Music Playing, Keep Dancing

FRIDAY · EARLY AFTERNOON The crossroads between JYP Entertainment and ShiningYouth Two trainees, one young president, and a very fast change in honorifics

Minatozaki Sana and Hirai Momo stood where they were, both of them completely dazed.

In their understanding, an entertainment-company president was either a greasy, potbellied middle-aged man or a severe, properly dressed middle-aged executive.

Someone like President Park Jin-young.

Song Ji-yeon, however, was absurdly young and more handsome than a male idol. Yet he was the president of an entertainment company.

Once the two girls came back to their senses, they instinctively stepped half a pace backward and bowed to him at ninety degrees in perfect unison.

As trainees who had not debuted, they were still very afraid of company presidents.

Especially entertainment-company presidents.

In their eyes, Song Ji-yeon was President Park in a younger, handsomer form.

“President-nim, we were terribly rude just now!”

Momo’s voice even carried a hint of fear.

Sana also put away her puppyish grin and lowered her head obediently.

Song Ji-yeon found it funny and waved them off.

“All right, stop bowing. I’m not your company president.”

Those two must be genuinely afraid of Park Jin-young at work.

“Hurry back to JYP. If you’re late, you might get scolded.”

“Yes, President-nim. Have a safe trip!”

The two bowed again.

Song Ji-yeon did not say anything more. He said goodbye, crossed the zebra crossing, and headed toward the ShiningYouth building.

“Momo-chan, Song Ji-yeon-oppa is an entertainment-company president. That’s amazing!”

Sana watched his back disappear, her eyes shining.

Momo rolled her eyes, grabbed Sana, and hurried her toward JYP.

“Sana, stop daydreaming about him and move! We’re about to be late!”

FRIDAY · EARLY AFTERNOON ShiningYouth Entertainment — third-floor practice wing Six women in heels, one Japanese flight, and a president who refuses to book a red-eye

Meanwhile, Song Ji-yeon entered the company and took the elevator directly to the third floor.

T-ara were scheduled to perform in Japan the following Thursday. He planned to go two days early.

One day would be for the women to rest, and one day would be reserved for concert rehearsal.

That meant they would leave Korea for Japan the following Monday. Only three days remained.

He had come to collect their identification and passport information.

As soon as he stepped out of the elevator, he heard powerful music from the practice room, accompanied by the crisp sound of high heels striking the wooden floor.

Song Ji-yeon went to the practice-room door and looked through the clear glass.

PRACTICE FLOOR · T-ARA — “SEXY LOVE” · SHININGYOUTH, THIRD FLOOR

Six women in everyday clothes and narrow heels move through the counts: step, turn, hip, reset. The wooden floor answers every strike.

The formation travels cleanly across the room. Every body hits the beat together, each member carrying her own shape inside the same machine.

Japan is four days away. The rehearsal does not look like a group waiting for permission to return.

REHEARSAL LOOK · PARK HYO-MIN “THE STAGE STARTS IN THE PRACTICE ROOM”

Hyo-min between counts — the casual practice floor still gets a full idol’s posture, polish, and dangerous legs.

Top — a fitted black short-sleeve rehearsal shirt, clean and easy to move in

Bottom — tailored black shorts with opaque coverage, cut for the choreography rather than the camera

Legwear — sheer black tights that keep the line continuous under the studio lights

Shoes — slim black rehearsal heels, already answering the wooden floor on every count

The Hyo-min setting: composed between runs, stage-ready even when the audience is only a mirrored wall

Rehearsal rule: the concert begins long before the curtain rises. Every heel strike is a promise kept in advance.

The six T-ara members were dressed casually, but all of them wore thin high heels as they danced to “Sexy Love.”

At a glance, the room was nothing but long legs flashing from one count to the next.

Unfortunately, only Hyo-min and Jeon Bo-ram were wearing short shorts that left their slender thighs exposed.

The other four wore solid-colored track pants, giving Song Ji-yeon no chance to feast his eyes.

All six stepped, turned, and rolled their hips in perfect unison. It was a pleasing sight.

Each woman had her own presence.

No wonder people call them the visual group.

Song Ji-yeon leaned against the doorframe, his gaze moving over the six women before settling on Jeon Bo-ram at the far edge.

Bo-ram really did have it rough. In every song’s choreography, she ended up at the outside.

The public loved joking about her height: “Heaven above, earth below—Bo-ram is one meter fifty.”

From Song Ji-yeon’s angle, however, Jeon Bo-ram was simply short. Her actual proportions were excellent.

Her legs were straight and long, and her waist sat high. Among women of the same height, she had an unusually fine pair of legs.

And there was impressive substance hidden beneath Bo-ram’s small frame. Her overall line was full and shapely.

To Song Ji-yeon, Jeon Bo-ram was not a short-legged idol with a poor figure.

She was the finest kind of baby-faced beauty.

As an aside, if he had to name an idol with genuinely short legs and a truly five-to-five body ratio, he would choose Bae Joo-hyun from neighboring SM Entertainment, who had not debuted yet.

That woman really did rely on her face to conquer the world.

Just then, the music reached the final beat.

Park Ji-yeon turned toward the door and caught sight of Song Ji-yeon outside. Her eyes lit up instantly, and her movements grew even lighter.

The music stopped.

“Oppa!”

Park Ji-yeon wiped the sweat from her forehead and jogged straight toward the door.

The other five members stopped as well. Her shout startled them, and they all turned to look.

When they saw Song Ji-yeon outside, they were puzzled.

Since when had this little dragon become so close to him?

He was only here to inspect the company. Why was she so excited?

Lee Qri walked over to Ham Eun-jung and bumped her shoulder against Eun-jung’s.

“Eun-jung, when did Ji-yeon get so close to Song Ji-yeon?”

Eun-jung did not think there was anything strange about it. Ji-yeon and oppa had always interacted this way, and she had never found it unusual.

She had long since grown used to it.

“Hasn’t Ji-yeon always been like this?”

Qri frowned. Something did not seem simple.

In the past, Ji-yeon had been close to Song Ji-yeon but still maintained a proper distance between a man and a woman. Now, watching the little dragon continually try to press against him, Qri felt that something serious was wrong.

Song Ji-yeon saw the dragon run over, pushed the door open, and smiled at her.

“Weren’t your feet hurting? You seem to be running pretty well.”

Park Ji-yeon did not show the slightest restraint. She came up beside him in her heels and whispered proudly:

“Oppa, was my dancing good? I didn’t miss a single position.”

Song Ji-yeon rubbed her head and smiled.

“It was good. You deserve praise.”

Park Ji-yeon immediately looked pleased with herself and winked at him.

Song Ji-yeon could only smile helplessly. He understood exactly what she meant.

Don’t forget our promise.

The two of them entered the practice room together. Song Ji-yeon clapped his hands and motioned for all six women to come closer.

“Come on, sit over here. I have something to say.”

The five members stopped fidgeting and gathered in a circle. Some sat cross-legged on the floor; others leaned against the barre.

Song Ji-yeon found a place among them and looked around at their faces before speaking slowly.

“The performance in Japan next Thursday is coming up, so it’s time to book the flights. I need your identity-card and passport information.”

ShiningYouth had only been established for a week. T-ara had not been trained by the company; Song Ji-yeon had bought the group out of Kim Kwang-soo’s agency. The management department had very little personal information for the six women.

That was why Song Ji-yeon had to ask for it himself.

At his words, all six women froze for a moment, then nodded together.

What else could he want identity and passport information for? He was obviously booking an international flight.

As a girl group that had debuted years earlier, they knew the process too well to ask unnecessary questions.

But precisely because they knew it so well, the same doubt surfaced in all six minds.

“President-nim, isn’t next Thursday still five days away? Isn’t it too early to book?”

Park Hyo-min tilted her head.

“Exactly. We usually fly over on a night flight the day before the performance. When are you planning to book this one?”

Jeon Bo-ram followed her.

The moment the words night flight came up, the women’s expressions grew uneasy. Lee Qri even pressed her lips together without realizing it.

Red-eye flights were practically the fate of every Korean idol.

With a concert approaching, they would fly out at midnight, land without even having time to catch their breath, and go straight into makeup, rehearsal, and blocking.

On a lucky trip, they might sleep for two or three hours. On an unlucky one, they had to memorize scripts and review formations on the plane.

It was ordinary life for T-ara.

In fact, it was ordinary life for every popular group.

Staying up all night was terrible for the body, and bad for the skin too. Hyo-min did not want another red-eye.

A concert should begin with a rested group, not six idols arriving from an overnight flight with choreography still running through their dreams.

Park Ji-yeon, who had fewer suspicions than the others, spoke directly.

“Oppa, do you need us to send you our passport numbers? I’ll look for mine…”

She began searching through her bag.

Park So-yeon raised a hand.

“There’s no need for everyone to search individually. President-nim, I have the identity and passport information for all six of us. Give me your email address, and I’ll send it later.”

So-yeon was T-ara’s leader that year. Work required her to keep the members’ identity and passport information, and it was already sitting in her email.

Song Ji-yeon nodded.

“All right. Send it when you can.”

“Yes, President-nim.”

So-yeon lowered her head and began composing the message.

INTERNAL · T-ARA JAPAN PERFORMANCE

The Four-Day Plan — Rest Before the Spotlight

Depart Monday · concert Thursday · no red-eye flight

Day One — Fly to Japan, check in, and do nothing. Rest and relax properly.

Day Two — Enjoy Japan. The members get a real day off instead of sleeping through a hotel transfer.

Day Three — Rehearse, block the stage, test the sound system, and finish every practical preparation.

Day Four — Walk onstage rested and perform with clear heads.

“That way you can relax, and I can worry less.” — Song Ji-yeon

Song Ji-yeon thought for a moment, then looked at Jeon Bo-ram and Park Hyo-min.

“I plan to go to Japan two days early.”

“We’ll leave on Monday. On the first day, you’ll do nothing except rest and relax.”

“On the second day, enjoy Japan. On the third, rehearse, review the blocking, test the sound system, and prepare everything properly.”

“On the fourth day, you’ll go onstage full of energy.”

“That way you can take it easy, and I can have peace of mind.”

For a moment, all six women were silent.

Then—

“Jinjja!”

Park Ji-yeon was the first to jump up. Her eyes narrowed into happy crescents, and she threw her arms around Song Ji-yeon’s neck.

He reacted quickly and pushed her face away before she could put on an eighteen-and-over performance in front of her unnies.

“Long live President-nim!”

“Oppa, you’re the best!”

Lee Qri and Ham Eun-jung exchanged a glance and smiled too.

“Thank you, President-nim.”

“Thank you very much, President-nim!”

Park Hyo-min slapped her chest dramatically.

“Oh my goodness, no more red-eyes! I could cry from happiness!”

Jeon Bo-ram said nothing, but the smile she could not suppress showed how much she appreciated the plan.

Song Ji-yeon laughed at their reaction.

“All right, all right. Stop flattering me.”

“Send me the document information quickly. I don’t want to find out later that there are no seats left.”

“Yes, President-nim.”

So-yeon answered efficiently and kept composing the message. As she sent it, she could not help muttering:

“President-nim, if you keep this up, we may never be able to leave ShiningYouth.”

“That’s fine. Sign with me. I’ll give you the best managers—and feed you too.”

The six women laughed even more loudly.

Park Ji-yeon threw herself at his arm and shook it.

“Then you have to mean it, oppa! We’re taking you seriously!”

“Fine. Take it seriously.”

Song Ji-yeon played around with them for a while longer. When the time looked right, he clapped his hands.

“All right, enough. Keep the music playing, keep dancing.”

“I have other work, so I’ll go first.”

“Goodbye, President-nim!”

The six women stood neatly and bowed together.

Song Ji-yeon waved and left the practice room.

FRIDAY · AFTERNOON ShiningYouth Entertainment — Artist Management Department Passports, hotels, and a president finally joining his own overseas schedule

He took the elevator to the fourth floor and went to the artist-management department.

Song Ji-yeon first forwarded the passport information So-yeon had sent him to Lee Hye-jin’s email, then began giving instructions.

“I’ve sent the identity and passport information for the six T-ara members and me to your email. Book an international flight for Monday. The management department only needs to send enough people to accompany the six women; leave two behind to hold down the office. Decide who goes and who stays. Arrange the hotels in Japan too…”

Lee Hye-jin blinked.

“President-nim, you’re going with them?”

Song Ji-yeon smiled.

“I’ve never been to Japan. I’m going to see the world this time.”

“Yes, President-nim.”

After finishing his instructions to Lee Hye-jin, Song Ji-yeon left the department and returned to his office.

FRIDAY · 1:55 P.M. ShiningYouth Entertainment — the president’s office Five minutes to the afternoon market, eighteen billion won waiting to become real

Song Ji-yeon sat down in the broad, comfortable leather president’s chair and looked at the clock on the wall.

It was exactly one fifty-five.

Five minutes remained before the afternoon session of the stock market reopened.

He opened the computer on his desk and logged into his trading account with practiced ease.

At two o’clock sharp, the Peninsula stock market’s afternoon session began.

BukwangPharm opened high and surged toward the ceiling again. But the struggle between buyers and sellers had already become fierce, and selling pressure above was visibly increasing.

Song Ji-yeon did not hesitate.

For him, cashing out at the highest point predicted by the forecast was the safest move. Greed would create risks he did not need.

He placed large sell orders in several batches.

Buying remained feverish. Small investors and trend-following funds rushed into the market, quickly consuming the enormous sell orders he had placed.

In less than ten minutes, every share in Song Ji-yeon’s position had been sold.

BUKWANGPHARM · FRIDAY AFTERNOON EXIT

PositionFully liquidated in under ten minutes

Available balance after costs₩30,000,000,000

Pure profit₩18,000,000,000

Next directionU.S. equities next week

When the final order matched, every share in the account became available cash.

After brokerage leverage interest, commissions, taxes, and other fees, the available balance settled at three hundred billion won.

A full one hundred eighty billion won in pure profit had officially landed.

Looking at the long string of digits on the screen, Song Ji-yeon leaned back and let out a long breath.

With that enormous capital base, he was confident he could develop Zero-Sugar Soju and ShiningYouth’s restaurant business before the end of the year.

He had completed the first stage of capital accumulation. Three hundred billion won was roughly one hundred fifty million renminbi.

With such a strong foundation, he would be even more at home in the stock market. The efficiency of making money make money would only increase.

Next week, however, he planned to shift into U.S. stocks. The Peninsula market was too small and no longer large enough for his appetite.

Now that the initial accumulation was complete, he naturally intended to harvest the fortunes of the great capital predators.

I wonder whether Eun-jung’s Prediction Card can forecast the American market.

If it can’t, I’ll have to come back to the KRX.

Song Ji-yeon was about to close the trading software when the phone on his desk suddenly vibrated.

Two characters jumped across the screen: Peach.

Song Ji-yeon’s lips curved. He picked up the phone and answered.

Phone · Sulli → Song Ji-yeon · Friday, 2:30 P.M.

“Oppa, do you have time today?”

“What’s up? Doesn’t the busy woman have a schedule today?”

Sulli had been busy all week. After eating with Song Ji-yeon and Goo Hara on Monday, she had gone overseas the next day, apparently to Paris for a fashion-magazine shoot. The time difference meant they had barely spoken over the past two days, and he had genuinely missed the little peach.

“Soo-jung’s birthday is coming up. I want to buy her a birthday present. Will you come with me, oppa?”

“If you invite me, of course I’ll come. But you’re back?”

“I’ll arrive at Incheon International Airport at six tonight. Come pick me up, oppa. Then we can walk around the night market together.”

“Sure. I’ll pick you up at the airport.”

Song Ji-yeon looked at the time. It was only two-thirty, so he nodded to himself.
