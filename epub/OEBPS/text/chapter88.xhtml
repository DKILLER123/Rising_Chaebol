Her body clenched around him, a hot, tight grip, and he groaned, a raw, guttural sound from deep in his chest. He thrust into her once, twice more, hard and deep, chasing his own release. Then he stiffened, his head thrown back, a vein standing out on his neck as he poured himself into her, a hot, pulsing flood that seemed to go on forever.

Eun-jung. My Eun-jung. Mine.

He collapsed against her, his weight a welcome anchor in the storm of her senses. For a long moment the only sound in the room was their breathing — ragged, slowing, settling into each other the way they always had to, every time, as if the rhythm had to be rebuilt from scratch.

I can feel his heart against mine. Thump-thump. Thump-thump. Slower now. Two clocks in the same room, quietly agreeing on the hour.

After a moment, he stirred, propping himself up on his elbows to look down at her. He brushed a stray strand of hair from her forehead, his touch impossibly gentle.

“Are you okay?” he asked, his voice a low rumble, still rough with passion.

Eun-jung could only nod, a lump rising in her throat. More than okay. She had been taken apart and put back together, and every seam of her was humming.
