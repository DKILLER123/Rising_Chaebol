Chapter Eighty-Six

A Girl’s Heart

MONDAY · EARLY AFTERNOON Cheongdam-dong, Seoul Galleria mall, underground parking

Song Ji-yeon stepped out of the mall with two shopping bags in hand, a smile he couldn’t quite suppress tugging at his mouth.

He slid into the Mercedes and dropped the Vacheron Constantin and Cartier bags on the passenger seat.

The long string of digits now sitting in his bank account had his mood somewhere in the stratosphere.

Spent two hundred million. Made back close to two billion. And walked away with two watches worth two hundred million — free.

This felt a whole lot better than making money off the stock market.

Stock-market money was, in the end, just numbers on a screen. The real thing, sitting in your hand — there was no comparison.

He started the car but didn’t pull out. Instead he picked up his phone, scrolled his contacts, found Sulli’s number.

MONDAY · EARLY AFTERNOON Cheongdam-dong, Seoul f(x) dorm, living room

Sulli was in the f(x) dorm living room, deep into Sunny on the TV with Krystal. The phone buzzed on the coffee table; she picked it up without looking — saw the caller, lunged for the remote, paused the movie, and answered.

“Hi, oppa.”

Only after she’d picked up and greeted him did the stupid thing she’d done the night before last come back to her — when he’d walked her back to the dorm — and her stomach gave a small, uneasy turn.

Why were my hand and my mouth so fast. Shouldn’t have answered.

Ever since that night she’d force-kissed him, she hadn’t dared reach out first. Not even the usual morning and good-night texts. Partly embarrassment. Partly… she was terrified of hearing oppa reject her, out loud, in his own voice.

They were clearly just friends. And she’d gone and kissed him like some kind of ghost had possessed her.

She was afraid oppa only saw her as a little sister. That he didn’t think of her that way at all.

And now oppa had suddenly called. If he asked about that night — what was she supposed to say?

Maybe just lie — say she’d meant to bite him, to teach him to keep his hands off Hara unnie. Or maybe just admit it — that she kind of liked him, that she’d been kind of jealous of Hara unnie.

So hard to choose. What do I do!

Song Ji-yeon, for his part, had no idea what was racing through the little peach’s head. He didn’t give a single damn about that night. A kiss had gotten him a billion-won rebate. A billion. For one kiss. If one kiss was worth a billion, he’d let Sulli kiss him a hundred times a day — no, a thousand — ten thousand wouldn’t even begin to cover it.

“Sulli-ah, do you have time tonight?”

Sulli panicked harder.

“Ah… no, I don’t think so. Vic unnie’s back from her schedule today, we agreed to have dinner together tonight. Why, oppa — you asking me out?”

Gulp. He’s asking me out. To confront me face-to-face, isn’t he.

No! Peach doesn’t want to meet you. This — us, just like this — is fine. Let’s keep this.

Good thing Vic unnie was back today and they were having dinner. Otherwise she wouldn’t have had an excuse.

On the sofa beside her, Krystal shot her a look. You take a call, fine — but you paused MY movie? And is what you’re saying even meant for my ears?

“Oh, nothing big. I just bought a watch, thought it’d suit you, figured I’d give it to you. You have time right now?”

“Ah?” Peach blinked, dumb. “What watch?”

He wasn’t about to tell her he’d picked it out for her at the Cartier boutique. He thought for a second and laughed.

“I went to buy a watch today and saw a women’s one I thought would suit you, so I picked it up. If you don’t have time tonight, I’ll bring it by the dorm now — I happen to be in Cheongdam-dong…”

“Of course I have time!!!”

Sulli hadn’t seen that coming. The little knot of dread evaporated; she bounced straight up off the sofa.

Gulp!

What does oppa mean? I kissed him — and he’s still buying me gifts?

Doesn’t that mean… he’s saying yes? He’s pursuing me?

The more she thought it over, the warmer her chest got. By the rules of the universe — a girl kisses a boy, the boy doesn’t pull away, then he comes back with a gift afterward — wasn’t that basically an unspoken yes?

“Good. I’ll bring it to your dorm now.”

He hung up. Krystal, watching her face cycle red, white, red again, couldn’t help herself.

“What?”

“Oppa bought me a watch. Told me to come down and pick it up.” Sulli’s head was a mess; the words came out vague, distracted.

“What watch???” Krystal was floored. Vic unnie said this oppa of Sulli’s was a con man. A con man who gives watches? That’s too absurd.

“Aish, I don’t know either. We’ll find out when oppa brings it over, won’t we?”

MONDAY · EARLY AFTERNOON Cheongdam-dong, Seoul f(x) dorm, building entrance

The black Mercedes rolled to a slow stop in front of the f(x) dorm building. Ji-yeon picked up the phone and sent Sulli a message.

KakaoTalk · Sulli

Song Ji-yeon

Here. Come down.

Today was Monday. Sulli’s Goddess Ability refreshed at the start of every week — touch her, make her heart flutter, and the Handbook would issue this week’s Tenfold Rebate Card.

That was the reason he was dropping the watch at Sulli’s first instead of Eun-jung’s.

Once he’d milked another rebate card out of the little peach, he’d go buy Eun-jung a watch too, deliver it tonight.

The watch was the excuse. The rebate card was the point.

Upstairs, Sulli’s heart skipped a beat the moment the message landed. She gripped the phone, equal parts eager and uneasy, brain still at war over what oppa could possibly mean.

Krystal paused the movie, watching her. “What’s wrong? Your face is so red.”

“Oppa… oppa’s here. Downstairs.” Sulli’s voice had gone thin. “He told me to come down and get something. Soo-jung, come with me!”

Krystal raised an eyebrow. Going downstairs to pick up a gift needed a chaperone now?

Eh. Fine. Without me, who’d spoil you.

She pursed her lips and stood. “I’ll come with you.”

She wasn’t going purely to keep Sulli company, either. She was, in fact, curious about the guy Vic unnie had called a con man — the same guy Sulli couldn’t stop oppa-ing about. She wanted to see for herself what kind of creature this Song Ji-yeon was.

The one who’d gotten the two people in the group closest to each other — Vic unnie and Peach — into a cold war over him.

They changed and headed out together, took the elevator down.

Out on the sidewalk, Sulli spotted the black Mercedes by the curb first — and Song Ji-yeon leaning against the passenger door.

Krystal recognized the car too. She’d seen Sulli get out of it downstairs once before, but it had been night, light bad — she hadn’t caught his face.

Today he was in a suit, not casual, and the whole picture hit different — sharper, more striking, like the CEO in a K-drama.

Sulli’s pace slowed. The little deer in her chest was in open revolt.

Ji-yeon saw them too. His gaze swept both girls at once.

Sulli in a white floral sundress, fresh and lively. The girl beside her in a plain tee and jeans — cold, beautiful face, aloof air. He recognized Krystal at a glance.

Huh — she really does look a bit like Lim Yoon-ah. In his previous life, the K-pop corners of Reddit had a running bit about it: Yoon-ah and Krystal could pass for sisters more convincingly than Jessica and Krystal — actual biological sisters — ever could. The only thing the real Jung sisters seemed to share was the family forehead.

Thinking back on the old fan-roast threads, the corner of his mouth twitched into a small, knowing smile. He raised a hand to the two of them.

Sulli took a breath, dragged Krystal over by the wrist.

“Oppa.” The greeting came out small, her eyes sliding off him.

“Mm.” He nodded, eyes moving to the girl beside her, asking what he already knew. “And this is?”

“Hi, I’m Krystal.” She opened first — her voice exactly as cool as her presence.

Krystal was, honestly, a little disappointed. She’d assumed that anyone Sulli was this gone over would be top-tier handsome. He wasn’t. Handsome, sure. But not her type.

“Hi. Song Ji-yeon.” He nodded back, the introduction done.

So this was the ice princess, Krystal. Cold as advertised.

He wasn’t planning to chat up the ice princess on first meeting. He pulled his gaze back to Sulli, reached into the car, and took out the red Cartier bag.

He held it out. “For you.”

Sulli took it with her heart in her throat. Krystal leaned in to look.

“What is it?” Sulli asked, barely above a whisper.

“Open it and find out.” A thread of laughter in his voice.

Sulli pouted and opened the bag carefully, took out the red box inside.

The moment the box cracked open, both of them went still.

Inside, lying on its velvet, was a delicate women’s wristwatch. The diamonds on the bezel threw out sharp little sparks in the afternoon sun.

“Cartier… Ballon Bleu?” Krystal’s voice was pure shock.

She recognized the brand. She recognized the price. Neither was small.

There was a wide gap between having a few hundred million won and wearing a hundred million won on your wrist.

And gifting it.

This was the con man Vic unnie had talked about?

She glanced at Sulli — looking, frankly, a little shabby beside her — and back at Song Ji-yeon, suited, opening with a Cartier like it was nothing.

She knew Sulli had no money. Every won Sulli had earned since debut was sitting in her mother’s hands.

For a moment, she couldn’t figure out what was left on Sulli worth conning.

Con her for sex?

A man this generous and this good-looking — he’d be short of women?

Krystal didn’t buy it for a second.

Sulli was just as stuck. She didn’t know the exact price, but the Cartier name she’d heard, and the shock in Krystal’s voice told her it wasn’t cheap.

“Oppa, this… this is too precious, I can’t accept it.” She tried to give the box back in a hurry.

She’d figured oppa bought a small gift — the kind of thing couples exchange, a few hundred thousand won. Not this. Not luxury on this scale. Her heart was sweet on it, yes — but this much, she couldn’t take.

The little peach was, at heart, a frugal homemaker.

Sulli refusing was exactly what he wanted — gave him an excuse to engineer the contact himself. He stepped forward, took the box back, lifted the watch out of its velvet.

“I bought it for you, so it’s yours.” He caught her wrist on purpose, tone leaving no room for argument. “Let me put it on you.”

His fingers met the skin of Sulli’s wrist. The warm contact sent a small jolt through her — her whole body went faintly rigid.

The sudden touch threw Sulli’s heart into a spin; she was already lost in a fantasy — what if this weren’t a watch, what if it were a ring…

And right then, in Ji-yeon’s mind, the Goddess Handbook gave a soft chime.

Goddess Handbook — Goddess Ability Triggered

TriggerContact with Goddess Sulli

RewardTenfold Rebate Card ×1

Weekly cooldown — refreshed Mondays. Spend ₩100M, receive ₩1B back.

Ji-yeon’s chest lifted. Got it.

His hands didn’t pause. Head down, he focused on closing the clasp.

Sulli stared at his face, so close she could count lashes — felt the cool of the metal and the heat of his skin meeting on her wrist — her cheeks lit up like she’d touched a stove.

“Done.” He stepped back, admired it.

Silver watch on her pale, slender wrist — sharp, fresh, perfect.

“Suits you. I thought so the second I saw it.”

Part of why he’d bought her the watch was the rebate card trigger — but the other part was real: the watch suited the little peach. He’d thought so on sight.

Sulli looked down at the silver-white watch on her wrist. For a long moment, she couldn’t surface. She opened her mouth — couldn’t squeeze out a single clean thank-you — managed only a small, sulky mumble.

“But… this is really too expensive, oppa.”

“A gift’s a gift. There’s no such thing as too expensive.” He waved it off with a laugh. “Alright — watch delivered. I should head back.”

Sulli blinked. What?

Delivers the gift and just… leaves?

Wasn’t it supposed to be — deliver the gift, follow up with a confession, and then we’d be together?

What kind of move was this. Little peach opened her big eyes very wide. Big confusion lived in them.

He was already reaching for the car door; Sulli scrambled after him.

“Oppa! You… you don’t have anything you want to say to me?”

“No? I just came to drop off a watch.” He pulled the door open, glanced back at her. “Head back up — your Vic unnie’s back today, isn’t she? If she sees me, you’ll be in for another lecture.”

Sulli had complained to him more than once about how Victoria forbid her from contacting him — so Ji-yeon wasn’t going to linger.

Run into Victoria, and I’d have to explain myself to her. I can’t be bothered.

Sulli stood where he’d left her, watching the black Mercedes pull away and vanish around the corner. Even after the taillights were gone, she was still standing there, the red bag hugged against her chest, her face wearing a small, sulky grievance.

“He’s gone and you’re still staring.” Krystal crossed her arms, grinning. “Drool’s about to hit the pavement.”

“Yah! Jung Soo-jung!” Embarrassed fury — oppa bullied her, Soo-jung bullied her, the world was unfair — Sulli swung at her, missing by a mile.

Krystal dodged it easily, eyes catching on Sulli’s face. Wait. Getting a gift was supposed to make you happy, wasn’t it? Why the jilted-wife face?

“What’s wrong? You don’t look happy about getting a gift.”

Sulli hesitated. Then she told her everything — the night before last, and the entire emotional roller coaster she’d ridden since. She ended on the question.

“Soo-jung, what do you think oppa means? He gives me a gift — such an expensive one — and doesn’t confess. What does that mean?”

“Hahaha!” Krystal couldn’t hold it in. “Sulli-ah — is it possible… your Song Ji-yeon-oppa just saw a watch that suited you, bought it, and gave it to you… with zero romantic feelings whatsoever?!”

“And also — you actually force-kissed someone — hahaha — Choi Sulli, are you trying to laugh me to death?”

“Aish! Jung Soo-jung — I’m not talking to you anymore!”

Sulli was already regretting telling her. As a teammate, she knew Soo-jung’s personality well — ice princess in public, pure goblin in private. What had she been thinking, spilling it to her?

Sulli stormed toward the dorm entrance, ignoring Krystal entirely.

“Yah! Sulli, don’t run — I haven’t even asked you what kissing feels like yet!”

The two of them bickered their way back up to the dorm — Krystal still pressing, Sulli already turning the day over in her head. What had today actually been? What did oppa mean? Gave me a gift then bolted — what kind of move is that?

Another rebate card. Another billion in.

Easier than the stock market.
