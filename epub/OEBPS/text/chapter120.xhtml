Chapter One Hundred Twenty

Sulli: I Love Hearing the Truth

FRIDAY · 5:00 P.M. ShiningYouth Entertainment — fourth-floor president’s office One invitation, one airport promise, and a dragon who is learning to be jealous

Song Ji-yeon had just begun getting ready to drive and pick up Sulli when the office door opened a crack from outside.

Park Ji-yeon’s small head slowly appeared through the gap. She peered into the room with such obvious secrecy that the stealth itself became suspicious.

When she saw Song Ji-yeon inside, she pushed the door open and smiled.

“Oppa, you’re here! I thought you had gone out again!”

Again?

Song Ji-yeon pursed his lips. The little dragon was incapable of hiding what she felt; every thought she had was written across her face.

From that single again, he reasonably guessed that Park Ji-yeon had come upstairs to check whether he had gone to see Han So-hee again.

“What? Are you happy that I’m here?”

She was still wearing her practice clothes, with a thin sheen of sweat at her temples. She had clearly just finished rehearsal.

Park Ji-yeon giggled, walked to the desk with a smile in her eyes, braced both hands against it, and leaned toward him.

“Oppa, the unnies want to eat grilled pork belly in Gangnam tonight. Eun-jung-unnie asked me to see whether you want to come.”

Of course she was happy he was here. If oppa had not gone to see Han So-hee, she could relax.

She had been afraid he would slip away to that little fox’s place again without saying anything.

“No.”

Song Ji-yeon had not expected her to have a real reason for coming, but he still refused.

“I have something to do tonight. You girls go without me.”

If he had not already promised Sulli, Song Ji-yeon would have liked to eat grilled pork belly with the six T-ara members. But he had already agreed to pick Sulli up at the airport.

A man could not go back on a promise he had made.

So he could only refuse.

Gulp! He refused?

Park Ji-yeon froze. Her expression changed, and suspicion rose immediately: after work, was oppa going to see Han So-hee?

The relief that had just settled in her chest rose straight back up into her throat.

Oppa really is a giant pig’s trotter. Han So-hee is only one person, while T-ara has six. No matter how you calculate it, he should choose T-ara. Is oppa bad at mathematics?

Unable to understand him, she pressed the question.

“What are you doing, oppa? You aren’t going to see Han So-hee, are you?”

“No.”

Song Ji-yeon smiled helplessly. He did not hide the truth.

“Sulli is coming back from overseas today. I’m going to pick her up at the airport.”

Park Ji-yeon was stunned for a moment, then pouted.

Inwardly, she could not stop complaining.

Oppa, are you the president of ShiningYouth Entertainment or the president of SM Entertainment? One of your own artists personally invited you to dinner, and you rejected her in one sentence, only to turn around and run happily to pick up another company’s artist.

Oppa is not only a giant pig’s trotter. He’s a shameless womanizer too.

As far as she knew, he had slept with Eun-jung-unnie and that little fox Han So-hee.

Now Sulli was joining the crowd too.

And what about Sulli? If she was coming home, didn’t her company have managers to pick her up? Why did she have to trouble oppa?

Could she be hoping to become sisters with me?

Gulp… maybe that would not be so bad. Sulli is my friend, after all. I might even accept it.

Aish! What am I even thinking?

Park Ji-yeon shook her head and hurriedly stopped the wild thoughts multiplying inside it.

She puffed out her cheeks and snorted.

“Fine. If oppa isn’t coming, then I’ll go with the unnies. You go pick up your Sulli.”

She did not argue any further. Song Ji-yeon had already promised Sulli, and refusing now would indeed be wrong.

Still, she was already imagining how she would deal with this shameless womanizer after returning from Japan. She would make sure he could not get out of bed, then see how he planned to bother other women.

That thought improved her mood considerably. Park Ji-yeon turned, opened the office door, and left without looking back.

Song Ji-yeon watched the dragon disappear beyond the door and smiled.

Let her think whatever she wants.

He stayed in the office for another two minutes. When nobody came looking for him, he took his car keys and left, rode the elevator to the underground garage, climbed into his black Mercedes, and drove onto the expressway toward Incheon International Airport.

FRIDAY · 6:00 P.M. Incheon International Airport — Terminal 1, B2 parking level B32, a suitcase pickup, and the girl who has been waiting for a ride home

Incheon International Airport was far from central Seoul. From Cheongdam-dong, the airport expressway took nearly an hour.

Sulli had gone to Paris to shoot a magazine cover, and an intercontinental flight could only arrive at Incheon.

The evening expressway held plenty of cars, but it was not congested. Traffic moved quickly.

At exactly six, Song Ji-yeon drove into Terminal 1’s underground parking garage.

He parked on the second basement level near the elevator. Just after he unfastened his seat belt, his phone vibrated twice in his pocket.

It was a message from Sulli.

KakaoTalk · Sulli ↔ Song Ji-yeon

Sulli

Oppa, my plane landed on time. I’ve picked up my suitcase at the baggage carousel. Are you here?

Song Ji-yeon

I’m here. The car is beside pillar B32 on B2. Take the elevator down after you collect your luggage. I’ll wait in the car.

Sulli

Got it. I’m coming right down.

Song Ji-yeon looked at the pillar number beside the parking space and typed the reply before setting his phone in the center console.

He leaned back in the driver’s seat to rest.

Sulli had spent three days in France. Although they had exchanged messages every day, the time difference and their work had kept the conversations short.

She had always been very forward with him, sharing the details of her life without hiding anything.

Song Ji-yeon understood what the little peach wanted, and he liked the directness in her.

He did not know whether she could accept his unfaithfulness. In his previous life, she had been famous as a pure-hearted romantic.

So, between them, Sulli was always the one pushing the relationship forward.

He neither encouraged her nor rejected her.

Uh… he seemed to be walking farther and farther down the road of becoming a scoundrel.

Song Ji-yeon gave a bitter laugh. He could not help it. Everyone loved beautiful things; he was simply a little more broad-minded than most.

A few minutes later, somebody tapped lightly on the driver’s window.

Song Ji-yeon unlocked the car and stepped out.

Sulli was standing beside the door.

AIRPORT RETURN · SULLI “THE LITTLE PEACH COMES HOME”

A three-day Paris shoot behind her, a suitcase at her side, and enough sweetness in the outfit to make the airport feel like a reunion.

Outer — a cream knit cardigan, warm enough for the airport and easy to fold over one arm

Dress — a pale yellow floral dress, light and soft beneath the cardigan

Shoes — white canvas shoes for the long terminal walk

Finish — a pale gray wool beret, loose hair, glossy lip balm, and a small crossbody bag

The little peach off schedule: travel-tired, bright-eyed, and already leaning into the person who came to collect her

Airport reunion rule: bring souvenirs, ask whether he missed you, and accept no answer that does not contain the word “yes.”

She wore a cream-colored knit cardigan over a pale yellow floral dress. White canvas shoes covered her feet, and a pale gray wool beret sat on her head. Her long hair fell over her shoulders, and her lips shone with a layer of lip balm.

Beside her stood a twenty-inch silver metal suitcase, with a small bag hanging diagonally across her body.

“Oppa!”

“You’re here.”

Sulli released the suitcase handle, walked up, and hugged him before letting go.

“Thanks for coming all the way to pick me up, oppa.”

“With a hug like that, the drive was worth it.”

Song Ji-yeon took her suitcase naturally and rolled it to the rear of the car.

Sulli opened the front-passenger door and sat down.

After putting away the luggage, Song Ji-yeon returned to the driver’s seat, fastened his belt, and turned on the cool air.

Although it was already the middle of October, the car was still warm.

Sulli took off her cardigan and laid it across her legs. After fastening her seat belt, she turned toward Song Ji-yeon with a bright smile.

“Oppa, did you miss me these past few days?”

Song Ji-yeon started the car and pulled out of the parking space.

“I have to admit it. Without you chattering beside my ear, I did miss you a little.”

“What? When do I ever chatter beside your ear?”

Sulli leaned back in her seat, secretly pleased.

It did not matter how he phrased it. He had missed her, and that was enough.

I missed oppa too.

“Was the shoot in France tiring?”

“Very.”

Sulli raised a hand and rubbed the back of her neck.

“It was cold in Paris, and the photographer wanted an outdoor shoot beside the Seine. Oppa, you have no idea how long I stood on the riverbank in a thin dress with the cold wind blowing over me. But the dessert shops there were excellent. I bought you macarons and handmade dark chocolate. They’re in the top compartment of my suitcase. I’ll give them to you when we get back.”

Song Ji-yeon steered up the parking-ramp exit.

“At least you remembered me. The trip wasn’t wasted.”

“You were the first person I thought of when I bought gifts.”

The car reached the expressway. Night slowly pressed down, and the streetlights became a long ribbon of light.

“Speaking of gifts, where are you planning to buy Jung Soo-jung’s?”

Song Ji-yeon had not forgotten the purpose of picking Sulli up. He was taking her to buy a birthday present for Krystal.

“When is Jung Soo-jung’s birthday? Is there still time to buy something?”

“There’s time. Soo-jung’s birthday is on the twenty-fourth, so it’s still ten days away.”

“Ten days? Then why did you decide to buy something today?”

“I only have today and tomorrow free. The day after tomorrow I start another schedule, and I’ll be busy from the fifteenth through the twentieth. I thought I should buy it today.”

Song Ji-yeon nodded. Idols really did have little free time once their schedules filled up.

“Where are you planning to buy the present?”

Sulli thought for a moment, then her eyes lit up. She turned to him.

“Oppa, let’s go to the Dongdaemun night market! It’s Seoul’s largest clothing wholesale market, and there’s a night market in the evening. We can buy snacks too. I haven’t walked around a night market in ages.”

Dongdaemun was Seoul’s clothing-wholesale center and a famous nighttime shopping district.

During the day, the area sold clothing and fabric. At night, the shops lit up and stalls filled the streets. Food, shopping, and entertainment were everywhere, drawing visitors from every country.

“All right. We’ll do what you want.”

Song Ji-yeon entered Dongdaemun into the navigation system and increased the speed.

Unfortunately, this was rush hour in central Seoul. Traffic crept forward inch by inch.

Even with Song Ji-yeon’s excellent driving, he could only crawl behind the stream of cars like a line of tortoises.

By the time they left the expressway and turned into the Dongdaemun area, it was already eight o’clock.

FRIDAY NIGHT · 8:00 P.M. Dongdaemun night market — Seoul Tteokbokki, fish cakes, sugar pancakes, and a birthday gift for Krystal

The sky was fully dark, but the whole street shone like daylight.

Neon signs overlapped in red, orange, yellow, and green. Vendors called out, rice cakes sizzled on hot plates, and the smell of fried food drifted from the stalls straight into their noses.

Sulli leaned against the car window, her eyes curved into crescents.

“Dongdaemun still has the best Seoul night view!”

“It certainly has a lot of flavor.”

Song Ji-yeon smiled. Even before they entered the market, he could smell the thick red-pepper sauce.

He found a parking lot. Sulli put on a mask, and the two of them got out.

A wave of heat carrying the fragrance of chili sauce met them. Song Ji-yeon’s stomach growled twice in response.

He had eaten only one bowl of beef noodles at lunch and was hungry again.

“Oppa, let’s eat something first and put a little food in our stomachs.”

Sulli was delighted. She tugged at his sleeve and pulled him inside, her steps light as a bird’s.

The first stall they stopped at sold stir-fried rice cakes.

The woman running it turned a panful of red rice cakes on the hot plate. The sauce bubbled and released a spicy fragrance that went straight to the forehead.

“Auntie, one serving of tteokbokki, extra spicy!”

Sulli bought a serving. She ate only two casual bites before handing the bowl and skewer to Song Ji-yeon.

“Oppa, taste it! This tteokbokki is really good.”

Song Ji-yeon accepted the skewer without hesitation. They were already close enough that worrying about shared saliva would have been absurd.

He speared a rice cake and put it in his mouth.

The rice cake was soft and chewy beneath the sweet-spicy sauce. It tasted better than he had expected.

They continued walking.

They bought steaming fish-cake skewers and carried several bags of freshly fried dumplings and chicken pieces.

When they turned a corner, the sweet smell of caramel floated toward them.

A stall was making hotteok, with two aunties working behind the counter.

“Oppa, hotteok!”

Sulli pulled him to a stop again and bought one. She blew on it, tore it in half, and handed him a piece.

“Be careful. It’s hot.”

Song Ji-yeon took it and bit down. The outside was crisp, and the sugar filling was hot and sweet.

“Good.”

He nodded around the mouthful.

As they went deeper into the night market, they bought more and more. Sulli took only two bites of each item before the rest ended up in Song Ji-yeon’s stomach.

Sulli was very happy. In the past, she had visited night markets with Vic-unnie and little Soo-jung. Idols ate very little, and the three of them could never finish much.

Even when they divided one snack into three portions, they became full before they could taste many different things.

This was the first time Sulli had visited a night market and sampled so much food.

She looked curiously at Song Ji-yeon’s stomach.

“Oppa, your stomach can hold so much! How can you eat all of that?”

Song Ji-yeon smiled. The snacks were small portions, and after his physical enhancement, his appetite had grown too.

All that food had only brought him to seven-tenths full.

They walked and bought their way to the destination of the evening: the large Migliore shopping center.

Migliore was essentially a clothing-focused shopping mall with countless stores, specializing in retail and wholesale hats, accessories, and clothing.

Sulli pulled Song Ji-yeon up to the second floor, where hats, hair ornaments, earrings, and other small accessories were sold.

Hat shops stood shoulder to shoulder. Baseball caps, berets, and knit hats filled the walls.

Song Ji-yeon had never had much of an eye for such a variety of things, so he simply followed behind her.

Sulli, however, was absorbed. She looked left and right, then stood before a display and compared the hats for a long time.

A saleswoman noticed how intensely she was choosing and hurried over to make a suggestion.

“Young lady, this beret suits you perfectly. You look beautiful in it—just like a television idol!”

Song Ji-yeon’s mouth twitched. Sulli was wearing a mask. How had the saleswoman even seen whether she looked beautiful?

She must have x-ray vision.

The saleswoman pointed toward Song Ji-yeon with a laugh.

“If you don’t believe me, ask your boyfriend. Doesn’t the hat make you look even more lively and adorable?”

Gulp. Boyfriend. Auntie, keep saying things like that. I love hearing the truth.

Sulli was visibly pleased and turned to Song Ji-yeon.

He smiled and nodded. As the old saying went, a beautiful person could wear a sack and still look good. Sulli was obviously that kind of person.

Once Sulli saw him nod, her mood improved further. She was no longer thinking about the hat. She was thinking only about whether oppa had just admitted she was his girlfriend.

Her face flushed.

“Please wrap it up for me.”

The saleswoman saw the order completed and smiled brightly as she carried Sulli’s hat to the register.

Sulli did not follow her. Instead, she kept browsing the rows of hats.

“You just bought one. You’re buying another?”

Song Ji-yeon remembered that there was already one of Sulli’s hats in the car.

Do idols really need this many hats?

Sulli shook her head with a smile.

“I’m choosing one for Soo-jung. She likes different styles of hats and has an entire cabinet at home just for them, so I thought I’d give her one. As for the first hat… I bought that because you said it suited me.”

Song Ji-yeon froze. He had forgotten that they had come to buy Jung Soo-jung a birthday present.

So that was why she had examined every hat and tried each one on so seriously. She had been choosing a gift.

He had thought she was shopping for herself.

“Oh. I forgot we came to buy her a birthday present.”

Sulli smiled without answering and continued to examine the hats. At last, her gaze fell on a milky-gray baseball cap in the corner.

The shape was firm, the brim low, and a small logo was embroidered across the front. It looked quiet and clean.

“This one! Soo-jung will definitely like it.”

Sulli’s eyes lit up. She picked it up and turned it over in her hands for a long time. She tried it on several times, and the more she looked, the more satisfied she became.

“Soo-jung is cool and quiet. She doesn’t like loud or flashy things. This cap will suit her best.”

Song Ji-yeon stood beside her and could not help smiling at how seriously she took it.

Sulli cared even more about choosing a present for her friend than she did about buying for herself.

She paid efficiently, carefully placed both hats in the bag, and carried them with the smile of someone who had found treasure.

“Oppa, do you think Soo-jung will like this?”

“You chose it. Of course she’ll like it.”

Song Ji-yeon took the bag from her and answered casually.

Sulli’s smile climbed high.

After buying the gift, they wandered through the night market again, strolling from one end of the street to the other. A cup of juice appeared in their hands, then another bag of snacks.

The night breeze cleared most of the fatigue from the day.

When they finally headed out, the moon had reached the center of the sky, while the noise of Dongdaemun continued behind them, still lively and bright.

Sulli carried the bag with the hats, moved closer to Song Ji-yeon, and asked seriously:

“Oppa, will you come with me on Soo-jung’s birthday too?”
