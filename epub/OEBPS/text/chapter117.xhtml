Chapter One Hundred Seventeen

The Little Dragon’s Promise

WEDNESDAY · NINE TEN P.M. Cheongdam Daewoo Royal County — Unit 101 A front door left open, one sleeping dragon, and a grievance two hours old

Song Ji-yeon stepped out of the elevator and walked toward his apartment.

He was about to enter the door code when he noticed that the door was unlocked.

He smiled and pushed it open.

Only four people knew the code: his parents, Eun-jung, and Park Ji-yeon upstairs. His father, Song Min-seok, and his mother, Kang Seol-ah, were not going to arrive at this hour. That left Eun-jung or the little dragon.

His guess proved correct.

The running shoes in the entryway had been abandoned at crooked angles. The living-room ceiling light was on. Across the sofa, Park Ji-yeon lay sprawled on her back.

AFTER REHEARSAL · UNAUTHORIZED SLEEP “THE GREY COCOON”

The little dragon at rest — which is to say, occupying the sofa as if it were a conquered territory.

Set — a loose dove-grey rehearsal tracksuit, soft enough for sleep and completely unsuited to dignity

Hair — long, dark and scattered over the cushion like it had lost an argument with gravity

Pose — one leg over the sofa back, the other hanging toward the floor, arms locked around a cushion

Face — cheeks relaxed, lips faintly pursed, every trace of the daytime menace temporarily powered down

The domestic form of the dragon: harmless while asleep, territorial the instant her eyes open

A sofa is not a bed. Park Ji-yeon has never accepted this distinction.

Song Ji-yeon could not help laughing.

The dragon’s sleeping posture was as gloriously careless as ever.

He walked over and pinched her soft nose.

“Yah. Wake up, Park Ji-yeon.”

With her nose obstructed, Ji-yeon’s eyelashes fluttered. She made an indistinct complaint, slapped his hand away, rolled over, and tried to continue sleeping.

He laughed again and tapped her forehead.

“Sleep demon, go sleep in your own apartment. Why are you lying on my sofa?”

“Mmm… stop making noise…”

Ji-yeon rubbed her eyes and opened them slowly.

Song Ji-yeon’s smiling face came into focus.

For half a second she only stared. Then the day returned to her in a wave. Her sleepy eyes flew open, and she sprang upright like a cat whose tail had been stepped on.

“Yah! Oppa! You remembered that you have a home?”

She grabbed the cushion behind her and threw it at him.

His reflexes were faster. He caught it, dropped into the single chair, and raised an eyebrow.

“Why are you biting me the instant you wake up? This is my house. Do I need to file a report every time I come home?”

“So what if it’s your house? Do you know what time it is?”

She crossed her legs and planted both hands on her waist. Her chest rose sharply with indignation.

He checked his watch.

“Nine ten. Isn’t that still early?”

That only made her angrier.

“Yah, nine ten! I waited for you for two whole hours?”

“You sat in my house for two hours just to see what time I came home?”

“Aish! Who said I was waiting for you?”

Heat touched her cheeks, but her posture remained combative.

“I came to demand an explanation! Where did you disappear to all day? You abandoned us in the practice room this morning and vanished by yourself. You didn’t even come back to the company in the afternoon!”

Song Ji-yeon smiled.

“Didn’t I tell you? I took So-hee to find an apartment.”

So that was it. The dragon had come to cross-examine him. She had a wider jurisdiction than the law.

“Ha! Find an apartment?”

At Han So-hee’s name, Ji-yeon’s temper climbed another level. Her beautiful eyebrows twisted together.

So-hee. He said it like they were close.

“Oppa, who are you trying to fool? Are Seoul’s apartments built in the middle of the wilderness? Can looking at one place take you from ten in the morning until nine at night?”

He pulled a face. Looking at apartments did not take that long. Other activities had consumed the rest of the day.

“We viewed places, chose a neighborhood, signed the contract, went to the shopping center for household supplies, and moved her things. Which part of that doesn’t take time?”

“Keep inventing things. I’m listening.”

Ji-yeon leaned toward him, narrowed her eyes, and stared directly into his face.

She had moved before. There was no way she believed that story.

“I told Eun-jung-unnie that you had definitely been lured away by that little fox with the surname Han. That idiot even defended you. She said you had something important to do and told me not to bother you.”

Ji-yeon kneaded the cushion between her hands.

“Unnie has let you fill her head with nonsense, but I haven’t! I finished rehearsal and ran straight home. I wanted to see how long it would take certain people to stop being bewitched by a fox and remember where home was.”

Her words fired out like a machine gun.

He was not angry. The furious little dragon was too cute.

“I’m home now. So?”

If Eun-jung had asked, he would have needed a careful explanation. She was his woman. Park Ji-yeon, on the other hand, had no claim on the private details of his afternoon.

Ji-yeon’s mouth opened and closed.

“You! You—”

That light, careless answer left her speechless. Her face reddened.

What did he mean, ‘so’? Who did he think he was? She had waited two hours for that face?

“Don’t change the subject, oppa!”

The more she thought about it, the more wrong it felt.

Why did Han So-hee, a little fox who had appeared from nowhere, get to occupy oppa for an entire day?

Why did she get to?

Because she was pretty?

The little dragon is not exactly lacking in that department.

A fire began to build behind Ji-yeon’s ribs. One conclusion became clearer with every second.

Oppa had been enchanted by the fox.

That had to be it.

It would not do. Oppa belonged to T-ara. He was something a little fox was not allowed to steal.

She, Park Ji-yeon, had never learned how to surrender after deciding that something was hers.

Eun-jung-unnie was too passive to protect her own man. That meant her little sister would have to do it.

If Han So-hee could do something, the little dragon could do it too.

Better.

She got to her feet, crossed the room in three quick steps, and sprang at him.

Song Ji-yeon had just reached for the glass of water on the coffee table when she landed in his lap, her legs locking around his waist.

He tipped backward, barely keeping hold of the glass.

“Yah, Park Ji-yeon! What are you doing?”

She gave him no time to recover. Her arms wrapped around his neck, and she looked down at him with a face flushed from anger and resolve.

“Oppa, if Han So-hee can do it, I can do it too! You’re not allowed to go see her anymore!”

He stared at the face inches from his own.

This plot has become increasingly abstract. Is that the official diagnosis?

He drew breath to answer.

Ji-yeon closed her eyes and lowered her mouth to his.

The instant their lips met, his mind rang. Water splashed out of the glass in his hand, and he did not notice.

She kissed him without technique, awkward and inexperienced, but with a reckless force that refused to leave room for rejection. It felt like a declaration of ownership and a challenge at once.

At last she pulled back, breathing hard. Her bright eyes still held a trace of embarrassment, but she forced a triumphant smile across her face.

“Well, oppa? Isn’t my kiss sweeter than that little fox’s?”

Song Ji-yeon was completely stunned.

It took him a long moment to recover. He looked at the little dragon still hanging from him, her expression proud enough to demand a score.

“Park Ji-yeon, what kind of nonsense are you doing?”

His voice carried genuine disbelief.

One woman had just slept with him, and he had barely finished calming her down. Now another had launched herself into his arms and kissed him by force of enthusiasm.

“Nonsense?”

Ji-yeon pointed at herself with one hand and kept the other looped around his neck.

“I don’t care what you say. You can’t go looking for Han So-hee anymore. If you do, I’ll tell Eun-jung-unnie that you kissed me tonight. Let’s see what she does to you.”

He blinked.

“You kissed me! And what would telling Eun-jung-unnie accomplish?”

Ji-yeon lifted her chin.

“Hmph. I figured out a long time ago that you and Eun-jung-unnie are involved. You’re not innocent either. You have Eun-jung-unnie and still want other women.”

Song Ji-yeon could not help laughing.

Eun-jung had believed she was hiding their relationship well. Apparently even the little dragon had seen through it.

The entire T-ara probably knows.

He fixed her with a serious look.

“If you know I’m involved with your Eun-jung-unnie, why are you doing this? Are you trying to tempt me into a crime?”

“Please. As if I had to tempt you for you to fool around with other women.”

Before today, he could have denied it. He had only been with Eun-jung.

Now that Han So-hee had joined his life, he was a man with two boats under him. Denial would be difficult to defend.

Ji-yeon watched him fail to argue and puffed out her cheeks. She had thought he would lie to make her feel better. He had not even tried.

“Fine, then. Did that little fox seduce you? Did you sleep with Han So-hee?”

There was no point in dodging anymore.

“Yes. Han So-hee and I slept together.”

“Aish! I knew that little fox was trouble!”

Ji-yeon’s chest rose and fell sharply. Her legs tightened around his waist without conscious thought.

He sucked in a breath.

“Yah! Loosen up. Are you trying to strangle me?”

“Good! You deserve to be strangled, you enormous jerk!”

She glared at him as if her eyes might catch fire.

It would not do. Eun-jung-unnie spent every day thinking about rehearsal and almost nothing about men. She was peaceful to the point of looking like a nun.

If this continued, oppa would stop belonging to them.

Ji-yeon’s mind began calculating.

Would shouting at him not be exactly what Han So-hee wanted? What if she pushed him completely into the fox’s arms? Then T-ara would lose without even putting up a fight.

No.

Oppa belonged to T-ara. He belonged to her. Nobody was going to take him away.

If Eun-jung-unnie was too soft to manage her own man, then her little sister would have to do it herself.

In less than thirty seconds, Park Ji-yeon had built an entire fortress of self-justification.

She looked at Song Ji-yeon’s helpless expression. The fire in her eyes faded, replaced by something more determined and competitive.

She no longer thought of him as a cheating man who deserved punishment. He was a victim who had been contaminated by an evil woman. She would rescue him.

Song Ji-yeon watched the girl in his arms grow suddenly quiet.

His back prickled.

She was about to bite me a moment ago. Why does she look at me now as if I’m prey?

He swallowed.

“Park Ji-yeon, what is that look?”

Her voice softened. One pale finger twisted in his shirt collar.

“Oppa… I want it too.”

“What?”

He looked at the dragon who had been writing an entire romance in her own head and found himself without words.

“Park Ji-yeon, clear your head. I’m with Eun-jung—”

“I know you’re with Eun-jung-unnie!”

She cut him off and waved the fact away.

“That’s perfect. Unnie doesn’t care. She won’t mind me sharing!”

He was genuinely numb now.

Since Ji-yeon had said it so plainly, there was little left for him to fear. But T-ara had rehearsals for the next two days. If he did something with the dragon tonight, she might not be able to get out of bed tomorrow.

He shook his head and pressed one finger to her forehead, pushing her already lifted head back.

She winced, slapped his hand away, and glared.

What was that supposed to mean? Was he rejecting me? Was Han So-hee prettier than I was?

“Aish, oppa, what are you doing? Why can Han So-hee have you but I can’t?”

He rubbed his forehead.

“You forgot that you have rehearsal tomorrow. If something happens tonight, you won’t be able to get out of bed.”

Ji-yeon eyed him skeptically. Then she glanced down toward his lower half.

“Is it really that powerful?”

She was not innocent. She had never dated, but she shared a room with Hyo-min, who had shown her videos and explained more than enough about the subject.

She knew a first time could hurt. But unable to get out of bed? Wasn’t he exaggerating?

Song Ji-yeon rubbed his forehead again.

The young dragon truly does not fear the adult one. I will have to teach her some respect eventually.

“Whether it’s powerful or not, you’ll find out after we return from Japan. Go home and sleep. You have rehearsal tomorrow. If you keep refusing to get into bed, you’ll see how I punish you tomorrow.”

Ji-yeon’s face lit up.

“Chincha? You mean you’ll take me after we come back from Japan?”

He did not answer. He stood while still holding her, because leaving her in that position for another second was beginning to threaten his own discipline.

She struggled in his arms.

“Is that what you mean, oppa? Say it!”

She would not stop until she got a promise.

Song Ji-yeon sighed. He could not outlast her.

He nodded.

Only after receiving the answer she wanted did Ji-yeon finally settle down.

He carried her upstairs, entered her apartment with the code, and brought her into the bedroom. Then he dropped her onto the bed, pulled the quilt around her until only her head showed, and tucked the edges down.

“I’m leaving. Sleep properly. I’ll come wake you every day.”

Ji-yeon’s eyes shone.

“Good night, oppa. Don’t forget our promise.”

He gave her a helpless smile.

How did this become a promise?

He pulled the quilt over her head, switched off the light, and left her room.

✦ ✦ ✦

Back in his own apartment, he took a cold shower to suppress the heat still running through him. Only then did he lie down and review the day.

First Han So-hee had offered herself to him. Then the dragon had decided to join the queue.

Both women now called the other a fox.

If they meet, will they fight?

The possibility was not far-fetched. There would be more conflicts as the number of women around him increased.

Still, this was what made life interesting.

He stopped trying to plan how every woman would get along. The river would reach its bridge when it reached it. He would take one step and see what came next.

Then he opened the Goddess Handbook.

The moment Ji-yeon had kissed him, it had already issued a notification in the back of his mind.

Goddess Handbook — Contact Reward

Goddess ContactedPark Ji-yeon (T-ara)

RewardGood Luck Card ×1

Single-use consumable. The scope of good fortune is random.

He had little expectation for the card. He used it immediately.

The Good Luck Card dissolved into golden light.

Nothing happened.

Song Ji-yeon answered a few KakaoTalk messages from Eun-jung and Sulli, switched off the light, and went to sleep.
