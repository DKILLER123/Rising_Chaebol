Chapter One Hundred Eighteen

Off to Japan

THURSDAY MORNING Cheongdam Daewoo Royal County — Unit 201 A wake-up promise, two suitcases, and one friend who has become territorial

Song Ji-yeon got up early the next morning, washed, dressed, and went downstairs to Park Ji-yeon’s door.

He and the little dragon had agreed the night before that he would wake her. T-ara had a flight to Japan to catch that morning.

He entered the code and walked into her apartment.

Park Hyo-min was bent over a suitcase in the living room, arranging the last of her things. Fitted jeans outlined her long legs, while a loose white shirt fell just low enough to cover her hips and leave her straight, well-shaped calves bare.

TRAVEL DAY · T-ARA DEPARTURE “THE SUITCASE EDIT”

Park Hyo-min before the airport — casual enough for a morning, polished enough to be recognized in one.

Top — a loose white button-up shirt, sleeves pushed up as she sorts the case

Bottom — fitted dark jeans, clean-lined and practical for a day of travel

Hair — long and dark, falling forward as she leans over the luggage

Carry — a carefully packed suitcase, travel documents, and the calm efficiency of a member who has done this before

The Hyo-min setting: relaxed, pretty, and fully capable of turning a casual joke into a dangerous misunderstanding

Airport rule: never leave packing until the last minute. Domestic rule: Hyo-min has violated this rule only aesthetically.

The sound of the door made Hyo-min turn. When she saw him, she straightened quickly.

“Oppa, what brings you here?”

“I came to wake Ji-yeon. She’s still asleep?”

He changed shoes as he asked.

“Yes. She was so excited after rehearsal last night that she stayed up late. She’s probably still dreaming.”

Hyo-min tucked the loose hair at her forehead behind one ear.

“Sit for a minute, oppa. I’ll go wake her.”

She started toward the bedroom.

“No need. I’ll do it. You finish packing.”

He stopped her and headed for the bedroom with the ease of a man who knew the apartment.

Hyo-min watched him move as if the place were his own. Something flickered in her eyes.

Oppa and Ji-yeon really are close.

Inside, Park Ji-yeon had wrapped herself into a silkworm, leaving only her face exposed.

She was sleeping soundly and muttering into the pillow. Even when Song Ji-yeon pushed the door open, she did not react.

He leaned down beside her ear.

“Little dragon, get up. If you don’t, the plane will take off without you.”

She rolled over and presented him with her back.

“No… five more minutes.”

“Who was the one who told me to wake her this morning?”

“Me… but I’ve changed my mind.”

She pulled the quilt over her head.

Song Ji-yeon sighed, lifted the quilt, and gave her bottom a quick smack.

“Yah! Song Ji-yeon! You hit me?”

Ji-yeon sprang upright. Her hair looked like a bird’s nest, and the collar of her sleep shirt had slipped to one side.

“If you don’t get up now, you really will miss the flight. Hyo-min is waiting for you outside.”

“I know, I know. Oppa, get out. I’ll be there in a minute.”

She rubbed her eyes and pushed him toward the door.

When he returned to the living room, Hyo-min had already dragged both suitcases to the entryway.

“Ji-yeon is up?”

“She’s up and changing.”

Song Ji-yeon sat on the sofa and picked up a magazine from the coffee table. He flipped through two pages.

Hyo-min poured him a glass of water and sat beside him.

“Oppa, are you going to Japan too?”

“No. I still have company business to handle.”

He drank and looked at her.

“How many days will you be gone?”

“About a week. We have several programs and an advertisement shoot.”

Hyo-min smiled and made it sound like a casual joke.

“What, oppa? Will you miss us?”

She had not expected him to take the question seriously.

“I will, a little.”

The smile froze on her face. Her fingers tightened around the glass.

She had not expected him to answer so directly. Her heartbeat suddenly sped up, and warmth crept into the tips of her ears.

“Oppa really knows how to joke.”

She lowered her head and pretended to drink, hiding the change in her expression.

“I’m serious.”

He had seen the color reach her ears. The corner of his mouth lifted.

Hyo-min did not answer. She only stared at the ripples in the glass, while one thought after another spread through her like circles on water.

She had always known Song Ji-yeon treated the T-ara members well. He bought them gifts, helped when they ran into trouble, and brought food when practice left them exhausted.

She had assumed it was because of Eun-jung — because he loved Eun-jung, and kindness spilled over onto the people around her.

Now she was no longer sure.

Could oppa feel something for me too?

She shook her head at once and threw the thought out of her mind.

It was impossible. Oppa liked Eun-jung-unnie. Hyo-min had no right to harbor an idea like that.

But another voice whispered back.

Then why did he say he would miss us? Why did he keep looking at your legs?

Hyo-min glanced down at her jeans. Her cheeks grew hotter.

She stole a look at him, only to find him reading the magazine as if his earlier words had been nothing more than a passing comment.

She felt relieved.

She also felt, inexplicably, disappointed.

“Hyo-min, where is Ji-yeon?”

He had been sitting beside her for several minutes, and the maknae still had not appeared.

“She should be out soon.”

Hyo-min glanced toward the bedroom.

The door opened at that exact moment.

Park Ji-yeon emerged in loose athletic clothes, her hair tied back, a pink suitcase in one hand.

She saw Song Ji-yeon sitting beside Hyo-min and narrowed her eyes.

“Oppa, what were you and Hyo-min-unnie talking about?”

“Nothing. Just a couple of words.”

He stood and took the suitcase from her.

“Let’s go. It’s almost time.”

Ji-yeon did not move. Instead, she leaned close to his ear so only the two of them could hear.

“Oppa, did you just say you’ll miss Hyo-min-unnie?”

He raised an eyebrow.

“What, are you jealous?”

“I am not!”

She pinched his waist.

“Don’t get ideas about Hyo-min-unnie. She’s mine.”

“Yours?”

“Yes. She’s my best friend. Nobody gets to steal her.”

Ji-yeon deliberately glared at him, then took Hyo-min’s hand and pulled her toward the door.

Hyo-min looked back in confusion, having no idea what had just been said.

Song Ji-yeon followed with both suitcases, watching the dragon’s suspicious back.

That dragon has a serious jealousy problem.

THURSDAY MORNING Cheongdam-dong — the curb outside Unit 201 A week in Japan, a careful farewell, and one plan that survived the flight manifest

The company van was waiting downstairs.

Ham Eun-jung, Park So-yeon, Lee Qri, Jeon Bo-ram, Park Hyo-min and Park Ji-yeon had all finished packing and were standing beside it.

Song Ji-yeon put the luggage into the back and walked over to Eun-jung.

“Be careful on the way. Message me when you arrive in Japan.”

“I know. You need to eat properly while we’re gone.”

Eun-jung straightened his collar. Her eyes were full of quiet affection.

They did not behave too intimately in front of the others, but nobody missed what passed between their eyes.

Park Ji-yeon watched from the side and pursed her lips. Her plan hardened.

When we come back from Japan, I will take oppa completely.

“We’re leaving, oppa!”

“Go on.”

He watched the women climb into the van. He stood there until it disappeared around the corner, then turned back toward the building.

When he opened his apartment door, his phone rang.

It was Sulli.

Phone · Sulli → Song Ji-yeon

“Oppa, what are you doing?”

“I just got back from seeing Eun-jung and the others off. What’s up?”

“Nothing. I just missed you and wanted to hear your voice.”

Her voice came soft and sweet, with the sugary drag of a girl who wanted to be indulged.

“You missed me already? Didn’t you see me yesterday?”

“Yesterday was yesterday. Today is today.”

“All right. What are you doing today?”

“Practicing at the dorm. I have a radio program to record tonight.”

“Oppa, since Eun-jung-unnie and the others are in Japan, can I come play at your place?”

“Of course. But don’t you have a recording?”

“I’ll go after it. It isn’t far anyway.”

“Wait for me at home, okay? Don’t run off.”

“Ne. I’ll wait.”

He set the phone down and was about to shower when it rang again.

This time the message was from Han So-hee.

KakaoTalk · Han So-hee

Han So-hee

Oppa, I’m awake. I’m hungry.

[a small, miserable face with both hands pressed to its stomach]

Song Ji-yeon

There’s food in the refrigerator. Heat something up or order delivery.

Han So-hee

I don’t want to move. When are you coming to see me?

He looked at the screen and smiled despite himself.

One night had passed, and she was already learning how to be spoiled.

Before he could answer, another message from Sulli appeared.

Sulli · “Oppa, I’m almost there!”

Song Ji-yeon rubbed between his eyebrows.

One woman was waiting at home. Another could barely move. A third was on her way over.

His life had become busy in the peculiar way only a life surrounded by beautiful women could manage.
